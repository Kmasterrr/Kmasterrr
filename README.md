# Hey, I'm George 👋

I work at the intersection of **AI, software engineering, and technical leadership**. I'm interested in what happens after a promising demo: how an idea becomes a system people can understand, operate, and trust.

My approach starts with first principles. Define the problem. Identify the real constraints. Choose the simplest useful design. Then measure what the system actually does and improve it from evidence.

### What I think about

- **AI systems that earn trust:** clear boundaries, observable behavior, meaningful evaluation, and human judgment where it matters.
- **Engineering discipline in an AI era:** strong fundamentals, explicit tradeoffs, maintainable systems, and the willingness to question fashionable defaults.
- **Leading through clarity:** turning emerging technology into practical direction, helping teams reason through uncertainty, and making complex ideas accessible.

I write and build to explore how AI changes the craft of engineering—and which parts of the craft matter more because of it.
